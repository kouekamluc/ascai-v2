# ASCAI Student and Sponsor Attraction Plan

## Goal

Make ASCAI Lazio feel immediately valuable to Cameroonian students in Italy and credible enough for Italian and Cameroonian authorities to sponsor.

## Positioning

ASCAI should not present itself only as a community association. The sharper positioning is:

> The trusted bridge helping Cameroonian students settle, study, belong, and advance in Lazio, while giving institutions a measurable channel for integration, student success, and diaspora cooperation.

## Student Value Map

Students will pay when the platform helps them avoid mistakes that cost money, time, or status.

1. Arrival support: residence permit, codice fiscale, health system, housing, transport, university enrollment, and first-month checklists.
2. Academic support: university comparison, scholarship deadlines, documents, peer tips, and reminders.
3. Community support: mentorship, groups, events, private dashboard, questions, and trusted contacts.
4. Advancement support: success stories, leadership opportunities, internship/referral channels, and institutional visibility.

The membership message should be practical:

> Pay dues because ASCAI reduces confusion, gives you a trusted network, and helps you move faster in Italy.

## Authority and Sponsor Value Map

Authorities and sponsors need a reason beyond goodwill. ASCAI should sell outcomes.

Italian institutions care about:
- Student integration and retention.
- Reduced misinformation.
- Clear communication with a hard-to-reach student community.
- Local civic participation and community stability.

Cameroonian authorities care about:
- Diaspora visibility.
- Student welfare.
- Trusted communication channels.
- Stronger Cameroon-Italy education relationships.

Private sponsors care about:
- Ethical visibility.
- Trust transfer through ASCAI verification.
- Access to students and families with real service needs.
- Measurable campaigns and referrals.

## Sponsor Packages

Student Success Sponsor:
Fund orientation, student guides, scholarship support, and mentorship programs.

Institutional Bridge Partner:
Use ASCAI as a communication and feedback channel between authorities and students.

Diaspora Innovation Partner:
Support verified services such as money transfer, translation, relocation, housing, internships, and professional development.
